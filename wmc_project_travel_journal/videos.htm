Travel Journal Videos

I have collected my videos from my visit to Mallorca for you here! Despite the poor camera quality, I am happy to share these moments with you. The videos showcase the beautiful footage and breathtaking views that I experienced during my trip. I hope that the videos bring you as much joy as filming them brought me!

HARBOR OF GOLD

Evening waves in Santa Poca

The clip glides across the harbor just as the sun slips behind the hills. Fishing boats bob in the orange light, and you can hear the soft splash of the Mediterranean against the stones. It is a calm, unhurried moment after a day of hiking and swimming, and you can almost feel the warm breeze that kept the camera shaking.

EDGE OF THE ENDLESS

Clear view of the sea in D'Arta

This breathtaking footage captures the crystal-clear waters of D'Arta's pristine coastline. The video showcases the stunning turquoise Mediterranean Sea meeting the rugged rocky shores, with sparkling sunlight dancing across the water's surface. The calm, transparent waters reveal the underwater beauty of this hidden gem, making it a perfect spot for swimming and snorkeling.

WAVES & WIND

From Port de Soller to Sa Calobra

This thrilling catamaran journey from Port de Soller to Sa Calobra captures the magic of Mallorca's most spectacular coastline. The video showcases the dramatic limestone cliffs towering above turquoise waters as the boat cuts through the crystalline Mediterranean. You can feel the exhilaration of the sea spray and the gentle rocking of the catamaran as it navigates the scenic route.

Bluewater Highlands

Evening waves in Santa Poca

The soft lap of waves against the quay and the warm, salty breeze give the scene a quiet, unhurried close to a busy island day. The camera threads past towering limestone cliffs into a sweep of turquoise sea; waves slam against hidden coves, sea spray flashes in the sunlight, and the catamaran's wake sketches a foamy ribbon—an exhilarating, panoramic passage that captures Mallorca's raw coastal beauty.
